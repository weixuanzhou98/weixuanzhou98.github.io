This study assesses three lane management strategies and examines an innovative CAV reversible lane strategy that leverages both vehicle eligibility and access control to increase capacity.The proposed strategy was evaluated on a UK motorway segment via microscopic simulation. The results indicate that implementing a CAV reversible lane on a section of a motorway significantly improves overall traffic efficiency in the dominant traffic flow direction.
