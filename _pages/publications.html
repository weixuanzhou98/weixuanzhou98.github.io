Ongoing Research

Developing a full-cycle CAV platooning methodology for mixed traffic on motorway

Developing a finite-state machine (FSM) based Full-Cycle Platooning Framework (FCPF) that models the continuous lifecycle of CAV platoons, specifically leveraging platoon formation and dissolution algorithms. The framework was implemented in SUMO using PLEXE extension, and adopted empirically calibrated IDM model for CAV behaviour modelling. [currently under review at IEEE T-ITS]

Robust Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control with delay compensation and smooth switching at communication interruption

Developing a predictor based method to compensate for the delay in V2V communication. Investigating the smooth and stable switch control between CACC and ACC at communication interruption using sliding mode controller.
